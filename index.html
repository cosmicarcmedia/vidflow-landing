What VidFlow does with an episode

Clips from long video

Cut a long video or podcast episode into short clips. Pro includes 120 clips and 8 episodes a month. Agency includes 500 clips a month and 8 episodes per brand.

Images

Generate images to go with posts. Pro and Agency.

Writing in the brand's voice

Captions, text posts, titles and descriptions, and blog posts are written from the episode and matched to the brand's voice. Every plan includes them.

A content library for each brand

Everything VidFlow produces lands in the brand's library. Tag content, and queues can fill from the tags.

Scheduling and publishing

Schedule posts or fill a queue, and publish to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X from the same place. If your posts already live in a spreadsheet, import them as a CSV.

A workspace for each brand

Each brand lives in its own workspace, so one client's content stays apart from another's.

Approvals and client links

Posts can require approval before they go out. Clients can connect their own social accounts through a shared link, without sharing passwords.

Automated flows

Pro and Agency can automate repeat steps with flows, and both plans include bulk tools.

Questions

Who is VidFlow for?
Agencies that run content for several brands, and any single brand, creator, or podcast with long video to work from. Starter and Pro cover one brand each. Agency covers many.
What platforms can I publish to?
Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X. Facebook, Instagram, X, and personal LinkedIn publishing are live. YouTube and TikTok are in beta: until those platforms finish reviewing VidFlow, posts to them from customer accounts publish as private.
How much does it cost?
Starter is $29 a month, Pro is $79, and Agency is $199. Every plan starts with a 7 day free trial, a card is required to start, and you can cancel anytime. The pricing page has the full comparison.
Do my clients need a VidFlow account?
To connect their social accounts, no. You send a shared link, the client signs in with each platform, and no passwords are shared. The client never signs in to VidFlow.
Who builds VidFlow?
Cosmic Arc Media LLC, a video and social agency in San Angelo, Texas. The agency runs its own client work on VidFlow.
