Help / Publishing and scheduling

Post statuses and failed posts

Updated October 2, 2026

Every post in Vidflow carries a status that tells you where it is in the publishing process, plus a separate approval state if approvals apply to it.

#How it works

A background process checks several times a minute for content whose scheduled time has passed and picks it up for publishing. Each targeted platform is attempted independently, in its own step, so one platform failing doesn't stop the others from being tried. Vidflow keeps a record of every attempt, per platform, and the post's status is worked out from those records. Published means the post is confirmed live on every platform you chose. If only some platforms went through, the post reads Partly published, and the per-platform result is kept so you can see exactly what happened on each one.

A post goes out within an hour of its scheduled time. If Vidflow can't send it in that window, for example after an outage, it holds the post as Missed and tells you instead of sending it late without warning.

#Post statuses

StatusMeaning
DraftNot yet scheduled or sent anywhere.
QueuedSitting in a queue schedule, waiting for its next open slot.
ScheduledPlaced on the calendar with a specific date and time.
PublishingActively being sent to one or more platforms right now.
ProcessingThe platform accepted the post but hasn't confirmed it's live yet. Where one platform is still pending, the label names it, for example "Processing on TikTok".
PublishedConfirmed live on every platform you chose.
Partly publishedLive on some platforms, not on others. Open the post to see which.
FailedEvery attempted platform failed. Nothing went out.
MissedThe post passed its scheduled time by more than the late posting window (one hour by default) and was never sent.
Needs attentionVidflow couldn't confirm whether a platform received the post. It will not send that platform again on its own.

#Approval is a separate dimension

A post also has an approval state, independent of the statuses above: needs approval, approved, or rejected. A schedule with Require Approval turned on will not dispatch an item until it reaches approved. See Client approvals overview and Approval status in your library.

#The per-platform breakdown

Open a post and look at the Publishing panel. It lists one row per platform you chose, with the state of that platform, the account the post went to, and the time:

Row stateWhat you see
PublishedA link to the post on the platform.
ProcessingThe platform has the post and Vidflow is waiting for it to go live.
FailedThe reason the platform gave.
Needs attentionThe reason Vidflow couldn't confirm the result.
Not sentNothing was sent to that platform yet.

#What each status asks of you

Processing. Nothing to do. Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube confirm a post a little after they accept it. The status changes to Published when the platform says it's live. If a platform never confirms within a day, the post moves to Needs attention.

Partly published. Click Retry failed platforms. Vidflow sends the post only to the platforms that didn't get it. A platform that already holds the post is never sent a second copy. Fix the cause shown in the row first, such as a disconnected account or an over-length caption, so the retry doesn't fail the same way.

Missed. Choose one of:

  • Post now sends the post at once to every platform that hasn't received it.
  • Reschedule opens a date and time picker.
  • Next open slot puts a queue post back in its queue. It appears only on posts that belong to a queue.

Needs attention. Vidflow sent the post, and the platform never said whether it arrived. Open the platform and look at the account named in the row. Then choose, for that platform:

  • I checked, it posted. Optionally paste the link to the post. Vidflow records the platform as published and never sends to it again.
  • It did not post. Post now. Vidflow asks you to confirm first: "VidFlow could not confirm whether this reached the platform. Check the account first. Posting now can create a duplicate." Choose Post anyway to send, or Cancel.
  • Reschedule sends it once, at a time you pick.

Published. The post is live and can't be moved. To post it again, use Duplicate as new draft.

The notification bell lists these posts too. Its Review button opens the post on its Publishing panel, where the buttons above are. The Content Library's Needs your attention view and the count beside it in the sidebar list every post that is Missed or Needs attention, so none of them waits unseen.

#What "failed" means

Vidflow publishes to every platform an item is set to post on, and it evaluates each platform independently. If every platform attempt fails, the item is marked Failed. If some platforms succeed and others fail, the item is marked Partly published.

Either way, the specific error for each platform is kept and shown in the Publishing panel.

#Retry a failed post

Click Retry Post on the item, in the Calendar's Schedule Post dialog or from the content card menu. A successful retry shows "Post re-queued" and the item re-enters the normal publishing flow. On a Partly published post the button reads Retry failed platforms and sends only where the post is missing.

#Media links never go dead

Media files are stored with signed links that expire over time. Vidflow re-signs those links at the moment of publish, not when the post was originally scheduled, so a post sitting on the calendar for weeks or months will still find a working file when its time comes.

#Email notifications

Your workspace can list up to three notification email addresses (Workspace Settings > Notifications). Vidflow emails those addresses when a post publishes, when a post fails, and when a post is Partly published, Missed, or Needs attention, according to your workspace's notification settings.

#Get better results

  • Watch for Partly published, Missed, and Needs attention, not only Failed. These are the statuses where a post may not have reached every platform. The Needs your attention view in the Content Library gathers the ones that wait on you.
  • Set all three notification email slots for anything unattended. A workspace you don't check daily benefits most from the publish/failure emails; leaving the list empty means a failure can sit unnoticed until you happen to look.
  • Fix the underlying cause before hitting Retry Post. Retrying a caption that's still over a platform's limit or an account that's still disconnected just fails again the same way. Read the specific per-platform error first, it names the platform and the reason.
  • Don't worry about content sitting scheduled for months. Media links are re-signed at the moment of publish, not when you originally scheduled the post, so a far-out schedule won't hit a dead link on publish day.

#Timezones

Schedules capture the timezone from your browser when you set them up, and times are shown to you in that local timezone. If you or a teammate schedule from a different timezone, keep that in mind when comparing what a given time actually means.

#Troubleshooting

A post is stuck on Publishing: this should resolve quickly. If it doesn't move to Published, Processing, or Failed after a few minutes, try refreshing, and contact support if it persists.

A post says Processing for a long time: some platforms take several minutes to confirm a video. If the platform still hasn't confirmed after a day, the post moves to Needs attention.

A post says Needs attention and I can't tell whether it posted: open the account named in the row on the platform itself and look for the post. If it's there, choose I checked, it posted. If it isn't, choose It did not post. Post now or Reschedule.

Partly published: open the post and read the reason on the row that failed. The rows that show Published are live and stay as they are.

Retry Post doesn't fix it: the error message names the platform and the specific issue. Common causes are an expired account connection (see the platform's connect article), a caption over that platform's limit, or a missing required video. Fix the underlying issue, then retry.

No email arrived for a publish or failure: confirm notification emails are configured in Workspace Settings > Notifications, and check spam.
