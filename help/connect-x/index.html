Connect X

Updated October 22, 2026

Connect X (Twitter) to publish text and media posts from Vidflow.

#How it works

Vidflow posts through X's v2 API using an OAuth 2.0 token that carries a rotating refresh token. Vidflow refreshes it automatically in the background well before it expires, and every refresh attempt is deduplicated so overlapping schedule and Post Now activity can't burn through the one-time-use refresh token by racing each other. When media is attached, it's uploaded to X first and the resulting media id is attached to the post; video uploads process on X's side asynchronously, which is why a video post can take a couple of minutes to fully appear.

#Connect steps

  1. Open Workspace Settings.
  2. Click the Connections tab.
  3. Click X (Twitter).
  4. Sign in on X's page and approve access. X always shows its login screen here, so you can connect a different or additional account even if one is already signed in on your device.
  5. You are returned to Vidflow with X listed as connected.

#What Vidflow can publish

Text and media posts to your connected X account, from Post now on library content and from scheduled dispatch on both content items and legacy queue posts.

#Limits enforced

  • Post text: 280 characters. Scheduled content items are automatically sliced to this length.
  • Media: 512MB hard cap. Video uploads process asynchronously on X's side, which can take up to about 2 minutes before the post finishes.

#Monthly X post cap

Each workspace has a monthly allowance of X posts, set by plan:

PlanX posts per month per workspace
Starter30
Pro100
Agency300
Trial30
  • Only an X post that publishes counts. A post that fails, or one that is held back, does not use any of the allowance.
  • The count shows on the Billing page next to your other usage meters.
  • When the cap is reached, the X part of a post does not go out and the post shows a message that the monthly X cap was reached. The post's other platforms publish normally.
  • The count resets when the next month starts, and X posting resumes then. You can also upgrade to a larger plan for a higher cap. See Usage limits and monthly allowances.
  • A workspace with no active plan or trial cannot post to X.

#Token lifetime and reconnect

X issues rotating refresh tokens, and Vidflow refreshes them automatically in the background, deduplicated so that overlapping refresh attempts cannot burn through the token family. Most connections need no attention.

Note

Note: Posting media requires the media.write permission scope. If your X account was connected before Vidflow requested that scope, media posts will not work until you reconnect. Text-only posts are unaffected.

If X revokes the authorization outright, Vidflow pauses that account and emails you. Reconnecting resumes anything waiting on your schedule.

#Get better results

  • Reconnect once if you plan to post media and connected X a while ago. If your account was connected before Vidflow requested the media.write scope, text posts work but media does not. A single reconnect avoids a confusing failure the first time you try to post a video or image.
  • Write to the 280-character limit yourself. Vidflow cuts a longer scheduled caption down to fit, but a caption you shaped to the limit reads better than one cut off mid-thought.
  • Give video posts a couple of minutes before treating them as stuck. X processes video uploads on its own side, so a short delay between posting and the post appearing is normal.
  • Keep an eye on the monthly X post count on the Billing page if you post to X often. See the monthly X post cap below.

#Troubleshooting

Media posts fail but text posts work fine. Reconnect your X account so Vidflow can request the media.write scope. This is a one-time fix.

My video post is taking a while to appear. X processes video uploads asynchronously. Give it a couple of minutes before treating it as stuck.

My media was rejected for size. Confirm the file is under 512MB. Vidflow enforces this cap before attempting the upload.

X connection stopped working entirely. Check the Connections tab for a reconnect prompt and sign in again.

My post went to my other platforms but not to X. Check the Billing page. If the workspace has used its monthly X allowance, the X part of the post is skipped with a message and goes out again only after the count resets next month.

X posts do not show metrics in Analytics. Vidflow does not collect X metrics yet. See Post performance.
