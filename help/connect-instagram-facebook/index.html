Connect Instagram and Facebook

Updated October 1, 2026

There are two ways to connect Instagram in Vidflow. Facebook & Instagram is a single Meta sign-in that links your Facebook Page and any Instagram professional account attached to it during the same flow. Instagram only signs you in at instagram.com and needs no Facebook Page. This article covers both.

#Which connection to choose

  • Choose Facebook & Instagram if you also want to publish to a Facebook Page, or if you want Instagram metrics in Analytics.
  • Choose Instagram only if you have an Instagram Business or Creator account and no Facebook Page, or you do not want to link one.

A workspace can hold both kinds of Instagram connection side by side.

#How the Facebook & Instagram connection works

Publishing runs through Meta's Graph API using your Facebook Page as the anchor. Instagram videos post as Reels through a two-step process: Vidflow creates a media container from your video, waits for Meta to finish processing it, then publishes the container. Facebook picks the right endpoint automatically based on what you're posting, video goes to the Page's video endpoint with a title and description, image goes to photos, text-only goes to the feed. Both platforms rely on the same stored Meta token, so a problem with one (like an expired token) affects both at once.

#What you need before connecting Facebook & Instagram

  • A Facebook Page. Vidflow publishes to Facebook through a Page, not a personal profile.
  • For Instagram, a professional (business) Instagram account linked to that Facebook Page. A personal Instagram account with no Page connection will not appear.

If your Instagram is not yet set up as a professional account linked to a Page, do that in Meta's own settings first, then connect in Vidflow.

#Connect steps for Facebook & Instagram

  1. Open Workspace Settings.
  2. Click the Connections tab.
  3. Click Facebook & Instagram.
  4. Sign in on Meta's page and approve access, including access to your Pages.
  5. You are returned to Vidflow. Vidflow links your Facebook Page automatically, and links Instagram too if a professional Instagram account is attached to that Page.

#Connect Instagram directly, with no Facebook Page

The Instagram only connection is for an Instagram Business or Creator account. It does not need a Facebook Page, and it does not link one. A personal Instagram account cannot connect this way.

Switch your account type first if needed. In the Instagram app, open Settings and privacy, tap Account type and tools, then tap Switch to professional account and choose Business or Creator. Switching is free and you can switch back later.

Connect steps:

  1. Open Workspace Settings.
  2. Click the Connections tab.
  3. Click Instagram only.
  4. Sign in at instagram.com and approve access.
  5. You are returned to Vidflow with your Instagram account listed as connected, marked (direct login).

If you are setting this up for a client, the same option appears on their connect link.

What publishes through it. Reels and single image posts, with a caption of up to 2200 characters.

What is not available. Instagram analytics. Vidflow does not collect post metrics for accounts connected this way, and Analytics shows a note saying so. To see Instagram metrics, connect through Facebook & Instagram instead.

Renewal. Instagram issues a token that lasts 60 days, and Vidflow renews it automatically before it runs out. You do not need to reconnect on a schedule. You will need to reconnect if Instagram revokes the authorization, for example when you remove Vidflow from your Instagram app settings or switch the account back to personal. Vidflow pauses publishing to that account and shows a Needs reconnect badge on it in the Connections tab.

#What Vidflow can publish through Facebook & Instagram

  • Instagram: video posts published as Reels, with a caption.
  • Facebook: video posts to your Page, with a title and description.

Both need a video file. Vidflow does not publish image-only or text-only posts to either platform today.

#Limits enforced

  • Instagram caption: 2200 characters.
  • Facebook title: 255 characters.
  • Facebook description: 5000 characters.
  • A video file is required for both platforms.

#Token lifetime and reconnect

The two connections handle tokens differently.

Instagram only tokens last 60 days and Vidflow refreshes them automatically, as described above.

Facebook & Instagram connections use a long-lived Meta token that Vidflow does not refresh in the background. When Meta eventually invalidates that token, whether from a password change, a permissions review, or normal expiry, publishing to that account fails and Vidflow pauses it rather than retrying indefinitely. You will get an email explaining that your connection stopped working and that reconnecting will resume anything waiting on your schedule.

#Get better results

  • Set up Instagram as a professional account linked to your Page before you connect, not after. Vidflow discovers Instagram through the Facebook Page's own settings during the same sign-in, so a personal Instagram account or one not yet linked to the Page simply won't appear, no matter how many times you reconnect.
  • On a Facebook & Instagram connection, reconnect proactively every few months rather than waiting for a failure. Vidflow doesn't refresh that Meta token in the background the way it does for other platforms, so a connection that's been quiet for a while is worth a check even if nothing has visibly broken yet. An Instagram only connection renews itself and does not need this.
  • Pick the connection by what you need from it. Instagram only is the shortest path for a Business or Creator account with no Page. Facebook & Instagram is the one to use when you want Facebook publishing or Instagram metrics.
  • Always attach a video. Neither platform accepts image-only or text-only posts here, so plan Instagram and Facebook into your content mix only for items that will have a video file by publish time.
  • Keep captions and titles inside the limits from the start, instead of writing long and trimming at publish time: 2200 characters for Instagram, 255 for the Facebook title, 5000 for the Facebook description.

#Troubleshooting

No connected account error when trying to post. You will see "No {platform} account connected. Connect one in Brand Settings." This usually means the Meta sign-in did not find a Facebook Page, or found a Page without an attached Instagram professional account. Reconnect and confirm you are approving access to the correct Page.

Facebook Page ID not found. This means the stored connection lost its Page reference. Reconnect your Facebook account from the Connections tab.

Instagram only sign-in fails or the account is rejected. Confirm the account is a Business or Creator account in the Instagram app, then try again. Personal accounts cannot connect.

Instagram is missing even though Facebook connected. Check that your Instagram account is a professional (business) account and is linked to the same Facebook Page in Meta's settings, then reconnect.

Posts are failing after previously working. Reconnect the account from the Connections tab. For a Facebook & Instagram connection, this is the expected fix once Meta invalidates the stored token, since those tokens do not self-refresh. For an Instagram only connection, it means Instagram revoked the authorization, so check that Vidflow is still listed in your Instagram app settings and that the account is still Business or Creator.
