Questions about plans and trials

What counts as an AI generation?
AI generations are a shared monthly pool. Captions, text posts, titles and descriptions, blog posts, and images each draw from it, and your plan sets the size: 150 on Starter, 600 on Pro, and 2,500 on Agency. Clips are counted separately on the plans that include them. The usage limits article in the help center lists what draws from the pool.
What happens when my trial ends?
After 7 days the plan you chose starts, and the card you gave at signup is charged that plan's monthly price. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged.
Can I cancel?
Yes, anytime, during the trial or after it. The billing article in the help center walks through how.
What does beta mean for YouTube and TikTok?
Both platforms review an app before they let it publish publicly, and VidFlow is still in that review for YouTube and TikTok. Until the reviews finish, posts to those two platforms from customer accounts publish as private. Facebook, Instagram, X, and personal LinkedIn publishing are live.
Do my clients need accounts?
To connect their social accounts, no. You send a shared link, the client signs in with each platform, and no passwords are shared. The client never signs in to VidFlow. Posts can also require approval before they go out, and the help center shows what a client sees when reviewing.
Can I run more than one brand?
Starter and Pro include one brand. Agency includes one and adds more at $49 a month each, with every brand in its own workspace.
